# FinPilot 10 x 40 Original Content Series

Date: 2026-08-10
Status: DRAFT — Level B proposal, human approval required before sale

## Scope

This proposal defines ten separate educational products. Each product is
planned as a minimum 40-page PDF, excluding optional cover, credits, and
resource pages. The pages are written from original FinPilot explanations,
fictional examples, exercises, checklists, and clearly labelled research case
studies. Source PDFs may inform the research, but no book is reproduced,
translated chapter-by-chapter, or sold as a substitute for its original work.

The ten products are deliberately different. A buyer should understand what
problem each one solves without needing to buy the others first.

## Shared editorial standard

Every product must include:

- a clear audience and prerequisite level;
- original examples or fictional datasets;
- a limitation or failure-mode section;
- no buy/sell/target-price language;
- no guaranteed-return, proven-edge, or live-performance claim;
- source references with rights status;
- a final “what this book does not prove” page;
- a version date and review owner.
