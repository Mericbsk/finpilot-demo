## Decision rule

A downloaded PDF is not an open-source work merely because it is accessible or
has no visible copyright warning. Commercial reuse, translation, close
paraphrase, figures, tables, and quotations require a verified licence,
public-domain basis, or written permission. Until that evidence is attached,
the product series uses original explanations, fictional examples, and original
exercises only.

## Required evidence before commercial adaptation

For every external work selected for adaptation, record all of the following:

- exact title, author, publisher or repository;
- canonical URL and access date;
- edition and publication year;
- licence text or public-domain basis;
- whether commercial derivatives, translation, excerpts, figures, and tables are permitted;
- attribution wording required by the licence;
- files or pages used and the transformation performed;
- reviewer and approval date.

## Current product conclusion

The ten manuscripts may proceed as original educational drafts only. No
external PDF is currently registered as an approved commercial source. The
absence of licence evidence is a blocking condition for sale, not permission
to reuse. This register must be updated with documentary evidence before any
source-derived text, translation, figure, table, or close adaptation is added.
